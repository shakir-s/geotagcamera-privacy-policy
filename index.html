Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GeoTag Camera uses, stores, and shares information on your Android device.

1. Information We Use

GeoTag Camera processes the following information only when you use the app's camera, location, preview, and gallery features:

  • Camera Data: Live camera preview and captured photographs taken when you press the shutter button. The app does not record audio or access the camera in the background.
  • Location Data: Precise GPS or approximate coordinates, horizontal accuracy radius, altitude (when available), and location timestamp/timezone.
  • Address & Map Details: Street address, city, state, country, postal code, and a satellite map thumbnail derived from your capture coordinates when enabled and online.
  • Photo & App Data Stored Locally: Saved JPEG images, embedded EXIF metadata (GPSLatitude, GPSLongitude, GPSAltitude, and DateTimeOriginal), gallery thumbnails, local photo records, and your watermark/camera settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

GeoTag Camera does not require an account and does not operate a developer backend server to collect your photos or location history. Your data is used solely to:

  • Capture photos and let you review or retake them before saving.
  • Fetch live GPS coordinates, reverse-geocode street addresses, and optionally render a satellite map thumbnail.
  • Draw a customizable location watermark onto your photo (when enabled in Settings) and write standard GPS EXIF metadata into the JPEG file.
  • Display, inspect, share, and manage your saved photos in the in-app gallery.

3. On-Device Storage, Retention & Deletion

All photos and app data remain on your device:

  • Saved Photos: Stored locally in your device's Pictures/GeoTagCamera folder.
  • App Database & Settings: Photo metadata records, small gallery thumbnails, and your customization preferences are stored in private app storage on your device. Cloud backup and device-to-device data transfer for this private app storage are disabled.
  • Temporary Cache: Unsaved capture files and temporary share copies are stored in the app cache and automatically pruned (within 24 hours for capture temps and 1 hour for share copies).
  • Deletion: Deleting a photo from the in-app gallery removes the saved image file, its thumbnail, and its database entry. Uninstalling the app removes private app data and caches; photos already saved in Pictures/GeoTagCamera stay in your device storage until you delete them.

4. Sharing Photos

GeoTag Camera never uploads or shares your photos automatically. When you tap Share, Android opens the system share sheet so you can send the photo to an app or recipient you choose.

Please note that shared photos may reveal your exact location through both the visible watermark overlay (if enabled) and embedded GPS EXIF tags. You can disable watermark fields in Settings before capturing if you prefer not to display location details on the image.

5. Third-Party Services

GeoTag Camera contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics or crash-tracking SDKs. External requests are made only for location, address lookup, and satellite map thumbnails when network access is available:

  • Google Play Services Location & Android Geocoder: Used to obtain device coordinates and convert coordinates into a street address. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps Static API (when configured): When a Google Maps API key is configured and your device is online, your capture coordinates are sent over HTTPS to request a satellite map thumbnail for the watermark card. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Esri ArcGIS World Imagery (fallback map tiles): When Google Maps Static API is not configured or unavailable and your device is online, map tile coordinates derived from your capture location are sent over HTTPS to fetch satellite imagery tiles for the watermark thumbnail. See Esri's Privacy Policy.

If your device is offline or you turn off Map Thumbnail in Settings, no external map requests are sent and GeoTag Camera generates an offline coordinate map card locally on your device.

6. App Permissions

  • Camera (CAMERA): Required to preview and capture photographs.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used to retrieve precise GPS or approximate coordinates for watermarks and EXIF metadata. You can still take photos without location permission, though location tagging will be unavailable.
  • Network (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to check connectivity and fetch address lookups and satellite map thumbnails over HTTPS.
  • Storage (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 8.0–9 only): Used on Android 8.0 and 9 to save photos to Pictures/GeoTagCamera. Android 10 and newer use scoped MediaStore storage without requesting storage permission.

You can grant, change, or revoke permissions at any time in your Android device settings.

7. Data Security

All external map requests enforce HTTPS (cleartext traffic is disabled), and private app databases and preferences are excluded from cloud backups. Please also protect your device with a screen lock and keep your operating system updated, as no storage or transmission method is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

GeoTag Camera is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or privacy practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
